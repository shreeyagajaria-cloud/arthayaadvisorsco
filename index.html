WHERE THE NAME COMES FROM

“Artha” — purposeful prosperity, not wealth for its own sake.

In Indian philosophy, Artha is one of the four aims of a well-lived life — purpose, prosperity, fulfilment, and freedom.

We took the name because it names what we actually do: pursue prosperity with discipline, grounded in your real numbers, built to endure across generations.
